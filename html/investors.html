The opportunity

A $500B+ global problem with no dominant solution.

Textile waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams globally. Municipalities spend millions on disposal while residents lack clear guidance. Sorty fills this gap with AI-powered decision support mapped to local circular infrastructure.

Business model

Dual revenue: B2G pilots + B2B licensing.

Municipal pilot contracts provide recurring SaaS revenue and diversion data. Corporate experiences and brand licensing create additional revenue streams. Community events build brand trust and generate proprietary training data.

Technology

Computer vision + geospatial AI — not a simple database.

Sorty combines on-device garment classification, condition assessment questionnaires, and geospatial mapping of circular infrastructure. This hybrid approach delivers higher accuracy than lookup tools and generates anonymized diversion analytics that municipalities and grant bodies require.
