The app

Meet Sorty, the next-best-use tool for unwanted clothing.

Sorty is a computer vision mobile application that helps users determine the next best use for unwanted clothing in under one second.

How it works

Simple decisions for circular textiles.

Take a photo

Users take a photo of a garment and answer a short set of questions about its condition.

Get a recommendation

The app analyzes the item and recommends the most appropriate pathway, such as resale, consignment, repair, donation, or recycling.

Find a nearby place

Sorty displays a map of nearby locations where the item can be taken, helping garments stay in use longer.

Watch a demonstration

An embedded demo area is ready for the final Sorty video link, with the app walkthrough framed for visitors and pilot partners.

Why it exists

Textile waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams globally.

Most people do not know what to do with unwanted clothing, and even when clothing is donated, systems are overwhelmed. It is estimated that up to 70% of donated garments are never resold, leading to export, downcycling, or disposal.

The gap

Municipalities need tools that help residents make informed disposal decisions.

Sorty was created to close this gap by providing a simple digital tool that helps individuals determine the next best use for their garments, prioritizing reuse, repair, resale, and recycling.

Decision support for textile circularity

Sorty is designed for people getting rid of one shirt and for systems trying to divert thousands of pounds of textile waste.

For residents

Understand whether an item is best suited for resale, repair, donation, consignment, or recycling.

For cities

Guide community members toward existing local infrastructure and reduce confusion at the point of disposal.

For industry

Build better data and better pathways for textiles moving through reuse and recovery ecosystems.
