Why it exists

Textile waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams globally.

Most people do not know what to do with unwanted clothing, and even when clothing is donated, systems are overwhelmed. It is estimated that up to 70% of donated garments are never resold, leading to export, downcycling, or disposal.

*"Source: Oxfam, 2023."*

The gap

Municipalities need tools that help residents make informed disposal decisions.

Sorty was created to close this gap by providing a simple digital tool that helps individuals determine the next best use for their garments, prioritizing reuse, repair, resale, and recycling.

How Sorty works under the hood

Computer Vision

Garment identification via image classification. Sorty analyzes photos to recognize garment type, material signals, and visual condition markers — giving users an instant starting point without manual data entry.

Decision Logic

Multi-factor routing based on condition, material, and location. A rules engine weighs reuse potential, repair feasibility, and local infrastructure to recommend the highest-value pathway for each item.

Local Data Layer

Live directory of circular options by postal code. Sorty connects recommendations to real nearby destinations — repair shops, consignment stores, donation centres, and textile recyclers.
